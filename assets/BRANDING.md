# UJ student-project branding

The original, unmodified logo is stored as `uj-logo.png` at its original square
aspect ratio. Source: https://news.uj.ac.za/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/UJ_Logo.png

Brand reference: https://www.uj.ac.za/brand/brand-fundamentals/

Orange, white and charcoal match the accompanying presentation. Burnt orange
`#A63C00` is used for small text and white-labelled buttons for contrast;
`#EA5500` is an accent. The logo is local; no tracking or external fonts are added.

Header, sidebar and About identify this as Hulisani Rambau's honours student
research prototype.

Deploy these files together:
- `streamlit_app.py`
- `assets/workspace.css`
- `assets/uj-logo.png`
- `.streamlit/config.toml`

Solving code, prompts, provider settings and saved study data are unchanged.
